# Three-Minute Demo + 30-Second Reflection

Record a single continuous screen capture at the public production URL: https://cuaderno-taupe.vercel.app. Keep the browser at a readable desktop width; briefly resize to a narrow mobile width near the end. Use the fictional defaults only; do not enter real candidate, employer, or personal information.

## Product walkthrough — 3:00

- **0:00–0:25 — Context and boundary:** Introduce First-Proof Pilot Economics and its target: a Mexican small-business owner checking whether human review is affordable. Point out “Fictional demo data,” “No data saved,” and the message that costs are not hiring evidence.
- **0:25–1:05 — Baseline inputs:** Show 12 candidates, 3 employers, 10 review minutes per candidate, $180 MXN reviewer hourly cost, $500 pilot budget, and $250 willingness to pay per employer. Explain that all are fictional planning assumptions.
- **1:05–1:40 — Explain the arithmetic:** Point to $360 total review cost, $30 per candidate, $120 average per employer, $140 budget remaining, and $390 modeled willingness surplus. State the formula: candidates × minutes ÷ 60 × hourly rate.
- **1:40–2:10 — Change affordability:** Change the budget from $500 to $300. Show “OVER PILOT BUDGET” and “Over budget by $60 MXN.” Restore defaults with “Reset demo.” Clarify that this is an estimate, not a hiring recommendation.
- **2:10–2:35 — Scenarios and stop rules:** Compare optimistic (75% time), baseline (100%), and pessimistic (150%) cards. Read the three proposed stop rules and state explicitly they are not validated findings.
- **2:35–3:00 — AI and human safeguards:** Show the “Simulated AI explanation” label and why no API is connected. Read the human-review/candidate-control safeguard and the prohibition on candidate fees, automatic rankings/rejections, and unpaid productive client work.

## Reflection — 0:30

Answer on camera in your own words: (1) What assumption most affects the cost? (2) What evidence would you need from actual employers and candidates before trusting this model? (3) What did you intentionally keep under human control? Do not present the fictional values or proposed stop rules as pilot findings.
